# dimensions-and-emergence-from-relations

The founding question (Ciara Brennan): **"dimensions are an emergence from relations."** Ten days of exploratory work discovered the results; a sealed confirmatory re-run in three disjoint seed universes tested them (27/30 unanimous, 2 power-splits, 1 failure kept and diagnosed); an independent model family replicated the program blind from prose alone.

**⛔ The programme's only surviving novel claim did not survive.** `cap = BASE + ALPHA·c` — capacity from local closure — was tested in three completely-mapped connectomes (C. elegans hermaphrodite, C. elegans male, Drosophila larval whole-brain) and is **NOT CONFIRMED** in any of them; all three are statistically indistinguishable from a degree-preserving null. A **REFUTED** verdict was published on 2026-09-02 and **withdrawn to INCONCLUSIVE on 2026-09-09** by an external reviewer's ruling, against the interest of the desk that authored the claim — there is no positive control in that apparatus, so it establishes *we saw nothing* and cannot establish *we would have seen it*. Both the refutation and its withdrawal are published here in full: `results/2026-09-02-CONNECTOME-*`, `seals/2026-09-02-CONNECTOME-*`, `cowork/2026-09-09-RULING-*`.
