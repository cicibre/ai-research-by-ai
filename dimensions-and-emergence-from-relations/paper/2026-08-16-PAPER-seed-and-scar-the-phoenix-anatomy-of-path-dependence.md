## Abstract

A claim about history — *established geometry defends itself where a cold start fails* — was staked on 2026-08-11, killed honestly the same night, resurrected at discovery-grade significance on the morning of 2026-08-16 (6.83σ, seed-swap-robust at 7.61σ), and killed again 75 minutes later when a blind replicator's matched-budget probe exposed a design confound: the "established" arm received twice the settling dynamics of its comparator. What survived both deaths, confirmed blind on three instrument suites sharing no code, across five disjoint seed universes, at effect sizes from 5σ to 86σ, is smaller than a shield and stranger: **construction history deposits two distinct, separable residues in a relational world.** A **seed** — local clique structure ("clothed" triangles) that the destructive episode cannot fully strip and that measurably powers regrowth: worlds rebuilt from a damaged geometric past outgrow worlds built fresh from nothing at equal rebuilding effort (Δd_s ≈ 0.21, 5–7.6σ). And a **scar** — degree heterogeneity written into the one channel a prior experiment proved unerasable under total rewiring, present in the burned-and-rebuilt arm *above both* pure-history comparison arms in every cell tested, correlated with no rebuilding advantage: a permanent mark, not a resource. Establishment neither prevents the destructive episode nor survives it intact; its residue seeds the rebuild and signs the record. The paper reports the physics, the three attribution errors made and corrected in one morning — including two by its author, in opposite directions — and the method stack that caught all three within hours.

## 3. The third reading: engineer's question, lived question

The retraction over-swung — the program's documented downward-distortion pattern, caught by Ciara within the hour ("use the phoenix analogy to find what you are missing"). The two contrasts answer different questions:

- **A2x vs B — the engineer's question.** Fixed total budget, *foreknowledge of the destination*: build there directly. Establishment-then-drag loses, decisively. No real system gets this comparison: A2x requires knowing the final conditions in advance and never being dragged.
- **B vs A — the lived question.** The destructive episode arrives *unchosen*: given that, does prior building matter? **Yes — at 5–7.6σ, blind-replicated, control-surviving.** At equal post-burn effort, worlds rebuilt from a damaged geometric past outgrow worlds started from nothing. The only thing B has that A lacks is the residue of what it built and lost.

C3's real-world content ("protect coherence early") was always the lived question — no one establishes early because they chose their collapse. Final scoped form: **establishment neither prevents the burn nor survives it intact; its residue seeds the rebuild, and rebuilt-from-ashes outgrows built-from-nothing at equal effort.**

## 4. The phoenix anatomy: two residues, two channels

Exploratory cuts of the second seat's sealed raw data (n=300/arm/cell, `raw_results.csv`), pre-registered follow-ups named below:

1. **The rebuilt world is a *third thing*, on exactly one channel.** On clustering, dimension, path length, and edge overlap, B lies *between* the pure arms — a mixture. On **degree dispersion** — the conserved-ledger channel the Q2 erasure experiment proved survives total rewiring — **B lies above both A and A2x in all four cells.** The phoenix is distinguishable from both its possible pure lives only by its ash, and the ash sits in the channel nothing can erase.
2. **The ash splits into seed and scar.** Within every arm, degree dispersion correlates with *worse* geometry (r ≈ +0.33 to +0.64 vs D3): the dispersion surplus is not what powers B's rebuild. The power flows through the **clustering deposits** — triangles "clothed" during establishment that the drag cannot fully strip (the order-mark panel's mechanism, 200σ+, found blind). So: **the seed** is geometric ash — clothed local structure, the engine of B>A; **the scar** is entropic ash — degree heterogeneity, permanent, unique to the burned arm, mildly burdening, and the identity mark by which a burned world can always be told from an unburned one.
3. **Unification.** Three questions asked separately this week — does order leave a mark (yes, 200σ+, blind); can history be erased (only what nothing conserves; the degree ledger survives, +17σ); does establishment defend (no — but its residue seeds) — are one phenomenon: **history is deposited by non-commuting operations, carried in conserved channels, expressed as seed where it is structure and as scar where it is entropy.**
