## Abstract

We report a confirmatory re-run of a ten-day exploratory computational program studying how construction history is stored, erased, and recovered in a minimal graph model. Every hypothesis, threshold, and analysis program was committed to version control before any data existed; the entire battery ran three times in disjoint random-seed universes; a claim is made only where all three agree, and a pre-committed clause forbids adding universes to break disagreements. Of 30 pre-registered predictions: **27 confirmed unanimously** (plus the world-validation gate opening in all universes), **2 split along a single pre-identified low-power cell**, and **1 failed unanimously** — the crash-size accounting, whose published diagnosis shows exact conservation under correct bookkeeping and a mis-specified sealed instrument, reported with the FAIL standing. The exploratory run, its errors, and their catches are Appendix A. We offer the protocol — sealing, self-tested producers, poisoned rehearsal, replication-tripling, a public plain-language notebook, and a refutation ledger — as a practiced answer to recent position papers calling for preregistration and falsification-first discipline in AI-assisted science.

## 1. Introduction

Two 2026 position papers ask for what this program practices: a preregistration regime for AI-generated hypotheses (arXiv:2605.08956) and falsification-first adversarial standards for agentic science (arXiv:2604.22080). Neither reports an implementation. This paper is one, run twice: an exploratory pass that discovered the hypotheses and made errors — every one caught by a structural mechanism and logged — and this confirmatory pass, in which the exploratory findings became sealed predictions and three of them sharpened or failed honestly under stricter instruments.

We are precise about claim types throughout. The throughput law below is a consistency identity latent in the model's update rules — the standard flux-balance argument of the self-organized-criticality literature — interesting here only because two independent research lineages missed it for days and because its residuals turn out to be a working instrument. The empirical claims (ordering, conserved-channel memory, the inheritance curve, identity constants, timing structure) are regularities of the specified dynamics, replicated across implementations and universes. No claim about any real-world system is made.

## 2. Methods

**Model.** Undirected graphs, N=900 unless noted. Three operators (seeded PRNG, dependency-free Python, verbatim from the exploratory run's committed source): *Generate* (random geometric graph, target degree 9); *Randomize(f)* (keep fraction f of edges, re-target the rest uniformly — conserving edge count and one endpoint degree per rewired edge); *Relax* (triadic closure balanced by lowest-overlap pruning; edge-neutral). A second model adds constant inflow K and clustering-dependent node capacities with probabilistic dissipation δ, producing accumulation-and-crash cycles.

**Protocol, in execution order, each gate committed:** (1) design extraction — the exploratory run's producers re-read file-by-file, every design transcribed with citations, seven record-keeping defects logged; (2) a master seal fixing all 30 predictions, bars, and seed universes, with deviations from the exploratory designs individually named; (3) analysis programs built against the seal, importing physics and the tail-grader from the exploratory run's committed files, with a 12-check known-answer self-test suite; (4) an error-recall pass against the program's documented failure classes (three defects found and fixed pre-data); (5) a dress rehearsal in a disclosed rehearsal universe whose outputs are *poisoned* — the grader structurally refuses rehearsal data outside rehearsal-labeled verdicts (three bugs found; eight minutes); (6) this paper's skeleton, committed with blank number slots; (7) execution in universes R2a/R2b/R2c (seed bases 200000/300000/400000), mechanical per-universe verdicts; (8) assembly. Two governing clauses: **no fourth universe** (disagreements are published, never re-rolled) and a **producer-defect protocol** (bugs found after data never move bars; both pre- and post-fix readings are reported). A plain-language notebook records every step before it happens and every finding after; the working cadence (plan → distance → error-recall → run → findings → reread the exploratory record → distance) was set by the director and held for all ten steps.

**Ancestry.** Registered reports (outcome-neutral criteria); blind analysis in particle physics; adversarial collaboration; and the ODD-based replication benchmark of Fachada et al. (arXiv:2602.10140), differenced from this work in §Appendix C (capability benchmark on known models vs. sealed discovery-and-confirmation protocol).

**3.1 The core replicates at scale.** World anchors landed within a third of a percent of the exploratory values (e.g., established clustering 0.7118/0.7118/0.7102 vs 0.712). Ordering gaps ran 15–30× threshold. The inheritance fractions — computed against bands sealed for an *external* replicator a day earlier — came in at 0.228/0.242/0.245 (f=0.5) and 0.374/0.316/0.334 (f=0.65), bracketing both the exploratory (0.201/0.327) and cross-vendor (0.210/0.306) values. The director's seed-swap control reproduced the main gap to the fourth decimal in one universe (0.0266 vs 0.0267).

**3.2 The power corner, pre-identified.** Both non-unanimous rows live in one place: the f=0.5 × spectral-dimension cell, the exploratory run's documented low-signal corner, at the seal's n=40 (the exploratory decisive runs used n=100). All effect signs agree with the exploratory run in every cell of every universe; nothing anywhere points the other way; the direct-build-wins result stands at ≈9.5σ at f=0.65 in all universes. The split is a sample-size statement and is reported as one.

**3.3 The inheritance curve, sharpened by replication.** ρ(severity) reproduced near point-for-point; monotone in all universes; inheritance at 80% destruction compatible with zero. The fitted exponent versus retained fraction: **1.82/1.85/1.89** — tightly consistent, inside the sealed band, and below the exploratory run's descriptive 2.28. The discrepancy is traceable: the exploratory sweep's analysis was never committed (its fit protocol is unrecoverable), while Run 2's committed protocol is reproducible by anyone. The exponent is protocol-sensitive at the ±0.4 level; 1.85 supersedes 2.28 as the citable value. One calibration, at a reviewer's insistence: the sealed band is ±0.5 wide, so the in-band PASS certifies "low-2s," not a value — the real content is the mechanism (triangle-survival, k≈2), and no more precision than the band delivers should be read into the row.

**3.4 Memory and identity.** Degree variance survived total rewiring at z=+10.8/+15.4/+12.6 with every non-conserved channel silent (MDEs: clustering ≈0.0009, path length ≈0.03). A +1.6σ sub-threshold clustering residual left open by an earlier referee did not reproduce (+1.60/+0.19/+1.08) — noise or conserved-channel shadow, resolved by replication. Identity: both re-identification ceilings perfect in all universes; aggregate shape at chance; and the surviving-relations constant at **0.4965/0.4958/0.4922** against the sealed band 0.485–0.501 — the fifth, sixth, and seventh independent measurements of a number two implementations agree on. Under continued dynamics, the inversion replicated: the conserved channel's mark erased by the middle horizon (1/0/1 of 20) while specific surviving relations still identified 14–16 of 20, and every channel died by the long horizon. The cycle counter (dispersion climbing ≈4.3→5.45 while functional geometry stays flat) replicated everywhere.

**3.5 The self-driving model.** Law 1's windowed instrument passed in all universes with every deviation sign explained by measured growth. The crash-size distribution: 28 of 30 per-run gradings POWER-LAW on the mid-body (self-tested CSN discipline), with the extreme tail remaining a distinct characteristic-scale species — the "not scale-free" scoping of the exploratory run, replicated. The load-gauge oracle — upgraded from the exploratory run's single world to ten per universe — passed ≥8/10 everywhere. Timing: sub-Poisson (CV 0.066–0.073); decoupled from crash size (corr −0.21…+0.02, where renewal predicts ≈0.9); **compressed below the independent-renewal baseline in all three universes** (CI uppers 0.91–0.96) — the one prediction sealed *at-risk* on single-analysis provenance, now three-for-three; and the clock survived crash-size capping everywhere. The rhythm of this model is budget-set: indifferent to discharge shape, tighter than independent renewal, unexplained by its most conspicuous feature.

**3.6 The unanimous failure, kept.** P8.2 sealed the crash-size law as: event sheds ×δ within 10–15% of the bracketed edge-drop, for all events ≥1000, ≥80% of the time. Result: **15% in band, identically in all three universes.** Diagnosis (published with the FAIL standing; bars unmoved per protocol): the sealed instrument counted only sheds in above-threshold ticks, while its measurement brackets necessarily include aftershock ticks whose sheds it ignored. Re-doing the accounting over the *same brackets* with the full edge budget (all sheds, plus inflow) yields median ratio 0.969 with 20/20 events in band — conservation is exact; the instrument was mis-specified. Compounding cause: the exploratory run's 89% figure came from an uncommitted analysis at a different configuration, so the seal extrapolated a bar beyond what the exploratory run had tested. A correctly-specified crash-size instrument is registered as future work. We regard this row as the protocol's central demonstration: a wrong sealed bar produced a loud unanimous failure and a transparent diagnosis, not a quiet band adjustment.

## 4. What this does not show
No law of nature; a toy model, one rule family, N≤1600, two implementations. The confirmatory pass is same-lineage as the exploratory pass (the cross-vendor battery of §5 — received and passed 2026-08-23 — addresses the implementation axis; the human external read remains the open evaluation-axis box). Exponent and floor-slope values are protocol- and size-sensitive at stated levels. Two further limitations belong here, where readers will look: the memory, marker, and identity results are one quantity — degree structure, conserved by construction — viewed three ways, and the non-trivial content is the magnitude and the partition, not the qualitative existence of memory; and twice in this program an uncommitted analysis set a sealed bar (the exploratory severity-exponent fit that yielded 2.28, and the 89% figure behind P8.2's band) — both alarms fired as designed, but bar-setting from uncommitted producers is the protocol's documented weak joint. The compression mechanism remains a surviving hypothesis with one ablation family behind it.
