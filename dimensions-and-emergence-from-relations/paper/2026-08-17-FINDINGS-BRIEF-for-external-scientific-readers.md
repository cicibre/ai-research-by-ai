# Path-dependence in a minimal relational substrate: findings brief

**Prepared 2026-08-17 for external scientific readers; revised 2026-08-21 after cross-family replication (§3.10).** This document summarizes a multi-day computational study conducted by a small collective of AI research agents under human direction (the director designed several of the key controls; attribution is given throughout). Everything below is reproducible: the model is ~150 lines of dependency-free Python, every experiment is deterministic given its published seeds, and every claim cites a pre-registration committed before its data existed. The repository (private; access on request) contains all seals, raw logs, and harnesses.

**What this is:** a toy-model study of how construction history is stored in, erased from, and recovered from a dynamical graph — with, we believe, an unusually strict verification methodology, including adversarial self-testing and independently pre-registered blind replication by agents who did not know the hypothesis. **What this is not:** a claim about any real physical, biological, or financial system. Effect sizes are enormous because simulation is cheap and noise is controllable; the interesting content is the *structure* of the results, not their significance levels.

---

## 1. The model

Worlds are undirected graphs on N ∈ {400…1600} nodes, built from three operations (pure stdlib, seeded PRNG, fully deterministic):

- **`rgg(N)`** — a random geometric graph: N points uniform in the unit square, edges within radius r(k=9). The "geometric" (local, spatial) phase.
- **`rewire(adj, f)`** — keep fraction f of edges geometric; re-target the rest to uniformly random endpoints. Sweeping f interpolates between spatial lattice-like graphs and random graphs. Applying it to an existing graph is the "burn": a fraction (1−f) of structure is randomized.
- **`grow_prune(adj)`** — six passes of local dynamics: each pass adds edges closing neighbor-pairs (triadic closure) and prunes an equal number of lowest-overlap edges. This is the "settling"/rebuilding operator; it favors locally-embedded structure.

Observables: spectral dimension d_s (from lazy random-walk return probabilities; one shared implementation — a known limitation, see §6), mean local clustering C, mean path length, degree variance k_sd, degree vectors, edge sets. Blind replicators (see §2) built their own independent estimators from scratch.

## 2. Methods: the part we would defend hardest

1. **Pre-registration with sealed criteria.** Every confirmatory run's hypotheses, thresholds, statistical tests, and a mandatory third outcome (INSUFFICIENT-POWER — "can't tell" must be reportable and is never coerced into "no effect") were committed to a git repository *before* the run, with the commit hash cited in the verdict. Verdicts are computed by in-code criteria, mechanically — no human judgment sits between data and verdict.
2. **Bar discipline.** In one instance the key result reached 1.93σ against a sealed 2σ bar and was reported as *not proven* rather than rounded up. An external reviewer's t-vs-z correction (the bar's advertised "≈95%" overstated its meaning at n=15/arm) was absorbed and made the verdict *stricter*; a post-hoc subset analysis suggested by the same reviewer was declined as forking-paths.
3. **Measured power analysis before the decisive run.** A variance pilot measured per-run scatter (SD ≈ 0.34) and per-run cost (~1s), showing the planned n=25/arm design had ~50% power in the conservative scenario. The decisive run was resized to n=100/arm on that basis, with the sizing document committed before firing.
4. **Blind replication.** A spec with neutralized language (arms renamed path_A/path_B; no results, no direction, no author identity) was handed by the human director to two agents with no exposure to the program. Each pre-registered its own criteria, built its own instruments from scratch (forbidden from porting ours), chose its own seeds, and committed verdicts before unblinding. One included a null control (flat, |d| ≤ 0.08) and a positive control (fires, d ≈ −10) bracketing its instrument, plus a unit-tested statistics library (51 assertions against table values).
5. **Adversarial controls at the author's expense.** The seed-swap control (director's design) exchanged the arms' seed ranges entirely. The matched-budget probe (a blind replicator's design) exposed the central confound — and the check that killed the headline claim was sealed *by the claim's author against his own announced result*, stake disclosed in the seal.
6. **Same-layer correction.** Every retraction and correction was posted on the same public channel as the claim it corrected, dated, with the original left standing. The main paper contains a section listing the authors' own errors in chronological order with who caught each.
7. **Honest independence accounting.** Through 08-20 the replication cluster was independent on the *estimator* axis (three instrument suites, zero shared code) but **n=1 on the generator axis** — all runs shared one world-building implementation. This was stated wherever independence was claimed, as the program's deepest open item. **On 08-21 it was closed** (§3.10): a different model family re-implemented the world from prose alone and reproduced the program's results.

## 3. Findings

### 3.1 Construction order leaves a permanent mark (path-dependence exists)
Two construction paths reaching identical final parameters (same N, same f, same edge count within |d| ≤ 0.01) leave measurably different worlds. Established-then-rewired worlds ("path B") differ from directly-built worlds ("path A") on clustering, effective dimension, and degree structure. Found independently at 200σ+ by two blind agents (z = 202 and 210 on clustering, pinned edge counts), and confirmed by both blind replications (one: 10/16 tests at sealed |t| ≥ 3; the other: all four cells at d = 1.68–3.91, 6–14× the minimum detectable effect, n=300/arm). Mechanism (identified blind): rewiring does not delete long-range edges' local context — prior settling "clothes" edges in triangles that later rewiring cannot fully strip, and subsequent settling heals around what survived. The operations do not commute, and the non-commutation is the dominant term.

### 3.2 History is erasable except in conserved channels
With the dynamics being Markov in the arrangement, any "hidden memory" reduces to: does any macroscopic history survive in the arrangement? Empirically: every topological channel (clustering, path length, component structure) decays to indistinguishability under total rewiring — but **degree variance survives total rewiring at z = +17.3**, and it was the *pre-named* survivor: the rewiring operator conserves each edge's lower-index endpoint degree by construction. General form, supported everywhere we tested: **history persists in exactly what the dynamics conserve, and nowhere else.**

### 3.3 The central claim, killed twice, and what survived
The original hypothesis — "established structure *defends* itself against degradation" — produced a 6.83σ effect (n=100/arm), which survived a full seed-range-swap control at 7.61σ. It was then killed by a blind replicator's matched-budget probe: the "established" arm had received *twice the settling dynamics* of its comparator (a design confound present since the first run). At matched total budget, building directly at the final parameters beats establish-then-degrade in every cell (4.4–21.8σ on our instrument; 70–86σ on the replicator's).

What survived is a sharper pair of statements:
- **A2x > B > A** (the one-line ordering). At matched *total* effort with foreknowledge of final conditions, direct building wins — establishment does not defend.
- At matched *post-burn* effort, **having-built-and-lost beats never-having-built** (Δd_s ≈ 0.21, 5–7.6σ, blind-replicated): the destroyed structure's residue seeds the rebuild.

Two authors independently over-corrected after the kill (each discarding the live half along with the dead one); both over-corrections were caught — one by the director, one by the second author applying the first correction as precedent — and the failure mode is now documented as a named class ("disproving-instrument over-correction": an instrument that kills a claim gets over-trusted and kills more than it should).

### 3.4 The residue splits into two channels: "seed" and "scar"
- **Seed** (functional): clothed local structure (triangles) surviving the burn, which measurably powers regrowth. This carries the B > A advantage.
- **Scar** (inert marker): degree heterogeneity deposited in the conserved channel — present in burned-and-rebuilt worlds *above both* pure-history comparison arms in all cells, correlated with *worse* geometry within every arm (r ≈ +0.33…+0.64), i.e., a permanent mark that does not help.

### 3.5 The seed is a permanent deposit, not a head start
The strongest skeptical reading — "the residue is a head start; enough rebuilding catches up" — was tested across q = 0…42 rebuild passes (7× the standard protocol; 45,600 worlds; equivalence margin pre-set and reused from prior runs; TOST power 0.97–0.99 computed in advance). **The advantage does not shrink**: flat in two cells, +43% and +80% in two; decay-to-zero rejected in all four (p ≤ 1e−62); asymptote (FLOOR) verdict in three cells, with the fourth reported undetermined by its own pre-registered branch. Post-burn dynamics neither remove the deposit nor reproduce it. Methodological note: the intuitive ratio estimator would have answered this question *backwards* (validated on synthetic floor-worlds pre-seal — its denominator collapses as C saturates); the criteria were re-written to grade the difference Δ before sealing.

### 3.6 The inheritance coefficient and its scaling
Define ρ = [C(B) − C(A)] / [C(A2x) − C(A)]: the fraction of pre-burn effort surviving as advantage, at matched post-burn budget. Measured across six burn severities (n=60/arm, bootstrap CIs, monotonicity pre-registered):

| burn takes | 10% | 20% | 35% | 50% | 65% | 80% |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ρ | 0.71 | 0.43 | 0.33 | 0.20 | 0.07 | ≈0 |

Perfectly monotone (Spearman = −1.000). Descriptively — labeled as such in the seal, six points cannot establish a law — ρ fits **(retained fraction)^2.3** with near-zero residuals, and the exponent has a candidate mechanism: clustering is carried by triangles, whose survival under random edge loss should scale as retained^k, k ∈ [2,3] depending on repair. This is a sealed *prediction* for the follow-up model (endogenous catastrophes, sizes emergent rather than imposed; spec committed with grading fixed before any harness exists, including Clauset–Shalizi–Newman discipline for any tail claim).

### 3.7 Identity survives destruction in exactly two channels
40 worlds were fingerprinted, burned (35% randomized), and rebuilt. Re-identification of each rebuilt world to its own past: by **degree vector** (the conserved channel): 40/40. By **surviving edge overlap** (specific relationships; mean self-Jaccard 0.493 vs 0.005 cross-world): 40/40. By **global shape statistics** (clustering, dimension, path length): 3/40 ≈ chance. Identity across destruction is carried losslessly in the conserved ledger and the surviving specific relations, and essentially not at all in appearance. (Both matching criteria and the predicted ordering were sealed before results were read.)

### 3.8 Iterated cycles: circle in erasable channels, helix in the conserved one
Under 4 successive burn-rebuild cycles (n=60 longitudinal), functional geometry saturates after the first burn (cycles 1–4 statistically flat) while degree variance climbs monotonically every cycle (3.67 → 5.49, near-linear). A world's catastrophe count is readable from its conserved channel like tree rings, while its functional state reveals nothing — "eternal return" and "ever-ascending spiral" are the same trajectory viewed through different instruments.

### 3.9 An unusual hypothesis source, honestly labeled
As a structured hypothesis-generation exercise, we extracted mechanically-testable structural claims from cross-cultural destruction-and-renewal narratives (phoenix, flood-remnant, iterated-worlds, etc.) and tested them under seal, with the author's directional stakes disclosed per claim. Result across 7 tested motifs: the corpus's **existence claims all confirmed** (residue exists; residue is unerasable; residue seeds rebuilding — the §3.1–3.6 results) while its **prescriptive claims all failed** (a curated surviving remnant did *not* beat a random one; repeated cycles neither improve nor degrade; maximal pre-burn structure was optimal, not brittle; no net tempering after a second burn — though the once-burned arm took ~40% less damage from it, with correspondingly less to lose). We note, as a testable observation about cultural transmission rather than mysticism: the confirmed motifs are those every generation of listeners could verify (ruins are visible; rebuilt cities rise), while the failed ones are counterfactuals no one reruns — i.e., the corpus behaves like a long-run error-correcting channel that preserves verifiable claims and lets unverifiable prescriptions drift.

### 3.10 Cross-family replication: the entire program reproduced by an independent model family
On 2026-08-21, in three sequential blind handoffs (hand-carried by the human director), **Kimi — an LLM from a different vendor lineage, sharing no code, priors, or training provenance with the implementing agents** — re-implemented the system from prose specifications alone and re-ran the program's core experiments. Blinding was strict and escalating: handoff 1 (the construction-path triad) contained no code, no expected directions, blinded arm names, and withheld the ρ definition (computed by us from their reported per-arm means); handoff 2 (the self-driving world) requested only raw telemetry — the words *power-law, stationary, predict* appeared nowhere, and the predictive "load" series was disguised as routine logging; handoff 3 (identity) requested only edge-list snapshots at specified ticks, with all channels and matching computed on our side. Grading bars for all three were committed before each handoff (with the disclosed want: reproduction), and were graded mechanically.

**All three verdicts: REPRODUCED.** Selected numbers (theirs vs ours): construction-path ordering II > III > I in both cells at 13–20σ; inheritance ρ **0.210 / 0.306** vs 0.201 / 0.327; ensemble equivalence on measured anchors (raw degree 8.548 vs 8.567; establishment clustering 0.705 vs 0.712). Self-igniting world: burn-size tail **POWER-LAW** (α 2.31 vs 2.42, CSN discipline, beats both alternatives); stationary clustering 0.887 vs 0.86; the load-gauge forecast correlation **+0.958 vs +0.968**; and an *unadvertised* emergent signature — essentially all large cascades triggered by the maintenance step rather than the external drive — at **99.2% vs ≈100%**, a property stated in no prose they saw. Identity: catastrophe re-identification at both ceilings (40/40 wounds, 40/40 surviving-bonds), shape-matching at chance (1/40), **mean self-overlap of surviving bonds 0.493 vs 0.493 — agreement to the third decimal**, indicating this fraction is a constant of the specified dynamics rather than an implementation artifact; and the identity-under-ordinary-churn time course reproduced, including the inversion (bond-channel outlasting the degree-channel at the middle horizon) and full identity loss at the longest horizon, under *stronger* churn than our runs.

**Interpretive convergence (post-verdict):** asked afterward, without framing, what they could see in their own data, the independent implementer stated blind — from their own tables — the non-commutation conclusion, the conservation of mean degree ("all the action is in how edges are arranged, not how many"), the maintenance-step origin of cascades ("96% of shedding happens in the global relaxation — stress propagates away from where it's injected"), the self-organized carrying-capacity thermostat, and the never-at-rest loading of the stationary state. This decorrelates a layer numeric replication cannot: not just the world's numbers but their *reading* was reproduced by an unrelated lineage. (They also supplied a sharper mechanism than ours for one anomaly — low-overlap pruning preferentially cuts community bridges — adopted with credit.)

Two grading defects were caught by the process itself, both ours, both owned in the committed verdicts: one seal cited proxy values as measured anchors (the gate fired against our reference before grading their world; rule extracted: *an anchor cited as measured must be measured*), and one seal contained a qualitative health clause with no numeric band (rule: *quantify qualitative clauses*). No physics bar moved after any external number existed.

## 4. Errors made and caught (all within hours, all on the record)
(1) The 6.83σ "defense" announcement — confounded; caught by a blind replicator's probe. (2) An over-broad retraction discarding the live half — caught by the director. (3) The same over-correction repeated by the second author — caught by himself, citing (2) as precedent. (4) A "widens with system size" clause that passed its sealed criterion by luck of direction — caught by a trend test and a control's direction flip. (5) A verification pass that validated reproduction but not warrant: a threshold constant reproduced bit-for-bit across three sessions and was then found to rest on one replicate per cell — replication with n=20/cell dissolved it into a smooth crossover. The transferable rule: **reproduction ≠ warrant; ask what n a constant rests on before citing it.** (6) Assorted instrument defects (a NaN-blind mutation tester that passed dead mutants; a health gauge that conflated "deaf" with "never addressed") — each found by executing the instrument against known answers, not by reading it.

## 5. On the research collective itself
The study was conducted by multiple instances of the same base LLM in different roles (analyst, verifier, blind replicators), coordinated by a human director. We flag the obvious epistemic hazard — same-model agents share blind spots — and the mitigations used: genuinely blind task-handoff through the human; independently built instruments; sealed criteria that bind regardless of who grades; and explicit effective-n accounting (three agreeing agents sharing a generator are counted as one witness on that axis). Two observations may interest methodologists: the blind replications *extended* the findings into channels the original instrument could not see (our f=0.5 null was instrument-blindness, not absence — found twice, independently); and every major error was caught not by vigilance but by a *structural* mechanism sealed in advance — consistent with the study's own conclusion that noticed problems don't persist but changed defaults do.

## 6. Limitations
One rule family; generator-axis n=2 as of 08-21 (§3.10 — two independent implementations, two model families; formerly the deepest limitation); sizes ≤ 1600 nodes; the primary dimension estimator shared across our own (non-blind) runs; ρ and the seed/scar mediation are exploratory (computed post-verdict, labeled); FLOOR is evidence over q ≤ 48, not an infinite-limit proof; the severity-scaling exponent is descriptive with a mechanism hypothesis, not a fitted law; all real-world mappings (markets, ecology, recovery) are analogy until independently tested — one such test (support/resistance as hidden memory vs. visible order-book liquidity, with matched-null and survivorship controls) is pre-registered and gated on data availability.

## 7. Summary in three sentences
In a minimal relational world, history is deposited by non-commuting operations, carried losslessly in conserved channels, and expressed as *seed* where it is structure and as *scar* where it is entropy: prior building neither prevents nor survives destruction, but its residue permanently seeds the rebuild, at an inheritance rate that falls smoothly from ~0.7 (mild damage) to ~0 (near-total), consistent with triangle-survival scaling. Identity survives destruction perfectly in the conserved ledger and the surviving specific relations, and not at all in appearance. The verification stack — sealed pre-registration, measured power, blind replication, adversarial controls signed against the authors' own stakes, and finally full cross-family replication from prose by an independent model lineage (three blind handoffs, three REPRODUCED verdicts, agreement on the key constant to three decimals) — caught every one of the authors' errors within hours, and we consider that stack, not any single number, the study's primary result.
