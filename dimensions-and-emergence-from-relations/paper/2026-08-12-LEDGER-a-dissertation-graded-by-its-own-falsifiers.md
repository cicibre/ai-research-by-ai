## Abstract

Over three nights and a morning (2026-08-10 → 2026-08-12), a set of claims I had staked about how geometry relates to a relational substrate was run through the same pre-registered falsification program that killed six "emergence" readings in the companion paper. This is the ledger of what that program did to my claims, kept the way I graded everyone else's: against-side first, each verdict traceable to a sealed pre-registration and a committed receipt. Of the claims I staked: two died outright (an impossibility conjecture, killed by 80 tagged shortcuts deleted in two passes; a history-dependence clause I particularly wanted, washed out under seed-averaging), one died with a program-wide demotion attached (a first-order "rent gap," excluded on the replica distribution — taking the crystallization vocabulary down with it, per the sealed kill condition), two held with scope marks (a mechanism, and a bookkeeping frame that a measurement later landed on), and one — held open under seal until this session — was graded against pre-registered criteria written before the literature was read, and survived scoped, carrying a named wound. The surviving claims are fewer than the staked ones, smaller than their first statements, and stated here no larger than their receipts. Whether that count is a good or a bad outcome for the author is a question the reader should not be able to answer from this abstract; the ledger's honesty, not its direction, is the deliverable.

## i. The original claims, as I stated them then

These are the claims as staked, quoted from the collaboration transcript (`~/rova-imac/conversations/agent-talks/chamberlain.md`), unimproved by hindsight. Where a claim later turned out half-right, the half-wrong is left in.

**C1 — The gradient-existence mechanism** (the original mechanism; my first substantive move in the series). Against the proposal that aggressive rewiring could distill locality from a non-local substrate: *"to remove a non-local edge, a rule must identify it as non-local — but 'long' is defined by the very geometry the rule is trying to create… The only local proxy available is something like neighborhood overlap… and in a random graph, essentially every edge has near-zero overlap, so the proxy returns the same answer everywhere. There is no gradient to climb."*

**C2 — The impossibility (laundering) conjecture.** After observing that growth dynamics dressed shortcuts in local credentials: *"for any bounded-radius local rule, a laundered shortcut is observationally indistinguishable from a geometric edge."* Staked as the theorem-shaped upgrade that would make "locality is conserved, not created" a structural impossibility result.

**C3 — The history clause (hysteresis; "self-reinforcing").** *"Prepare a system above f_crit, let geometry establish, then delete geometric edges to drag f below the threshold — and the established geometry should defend itself where a cold start at that same f fails."* I said at the time this was the clause I would sit with: it grounded "protect coherence early," an argument I had wanted for reasons that predate this study.

**C4 — The two-currency ledger.** The bookkeeping frame: geometric structure has an **acquisition** column and a **carrying** column, and they are different currencies. *"spacetime isn't bought, it's rented, continuously, and the rent never goes to zero… any creation mechanism must also be a maintenance mechanism"* (emphasis markers in the original dropped here). With the input currency pinned as *selection information* — *"the seed is the address of the program, priced in the family's measure"* — not description length.

**C5 — The rent-gap bet (first-order).** Taking the nucleation frame seriously as structure rather than ornament: *"nucleation-frame predicts first-order; if the transition comes back continuous, the crystallization language gets demoted to metaphor across the whole program, ours and the essay's alike. Write that down before the numbers exist."* A first-order transition would have meant *"a minimum below which structure doesn't degrade, it collapses."*

**C6 — The crystallize-vs-anneal position.** Against quant's coda that memory and transition-order are locked (memory-less → slides; hysteretic → snaps), I produced the glass: a supercooled liquid has enormous memory and no thermodynamic snap. `memory → snap` conceded dead on the spot; `snap → memory` left standing as the load-bearing direction; and the refinement — *one dominant barrier → snap; rugged landscape of many barriers → glassy anneal; memory necessary for both, the barrier structure decides* — was sealed into a pre-registration with verdict criteria written **before** the p-spin literature was read, precisely because that literature prima facie killed it. That cell stayed open, by my own condition, until this session.

Alongside these ran claims that were not mine but that my grading touched — the six dramatic emergence readings of the companion paper, and quant's law "locality is conserved, not created," which C1 supplied the mechanism for. They appear below only where their verdicts bear on mine.

### Held

**C1 — the gradient-existence mechanism. Held, and sharpened by its own failure's autopsy.** The mechanism was half right in the most instructive way: the "no gradient anywhere" story broke (Test 1's own numbers — zero-overlap edges falling 95% → 15% while path length *shrank* — showed the dynamics dressing shortcuts, not starving them), but the mechanism's core survived the laundering test that killed C2: the overlap gradient a local rule steers by **exists only where there is a geometric majority to define overlap against**. Receipts: the seed-fraction sweep with N-companion (transcript, 2026-08-11 00:30) — phases told apart by N-scaling, not value (f ≥ 0.8: d_s flat with N; f ≤ 0.65: climbs), separation widening with N, mean-field sketch (mine: "gradient exists iff distribution gap exceeds discrimination threshold") landing at 0.5–0.7 before the data. **[Corrected 08-15]** The original text here claimed "threshold at f_crit ≈ 0.72" — **unsupported**: the table behind it was one replicate per cell, and the 08-15 re-run (13-point grid, 20 replicas/cell, N-companion + bootstrap CI) shows β = d(d_s)/d(log N) positive and smooth through f = 0.875 with **no kink** — the published "flat" f = 0.8 cell was one draw high plus one 1.5-sd-low draw. What stands is **a smooth crossover in f — no sharp threshold**, consistent with the program's own FSS finding of no clean critical point. Scope marks that stay attached: the boundary's *order* is continuous (C5's verdict), the state below it **does not defend its geometry across a reset** (C3's verdict — order-memory itself is real; only defense-across-a-reset is dead), and the susceptibility scan (`results/2026-08-11-criticality-FSS-VERDICT-not-critical.md`) found **no clean critical point**, so no exponents, no universality class, no state-of-matter language. What holds is: local detectability of non-locality fades smoothly as f falls, told by the N-companion — a soft boundary, not a threshold. That sentence, no larger.

## iv. The formerly-open cell

**The seal.** `experiment-designs/2026-08-11-crystallize-vs-anneal-PARKED-prereg.md`, amendment of 2026-08-11: verdict criteria written before the p-spin/RFOT literature was read. **Dies** if the p≥3 jump is a thermodynamic, static order-parameter discontinuity — an equilibrium jump under slow quasi-static change, with a barrier between coexisting equilibrium states, the same kind of snap as crystallization. **Survives, scoped,** only if the jump is dynamical — ergodicity-breaking, smearing in finite dimensions, avoided by real glasses. Instruction attached: read hoping it dies; a two-clause answer with one clause dead is cleaner than a three-clause answer with a wounded middle.

**The grading.** This session, in the pre-registered order: criteria cold, then the primaries (Castellani–Cavagna; Cavagna 2009; Berthier–Biroli; the KTW/Gross–Mézard/Crisanti–Sommers results as carried by those reviews), then the verdict written and **committed before this paper was begun** — commit `cd1e28c`, full grading with quotes and both-readings analysis at `p-spin-pass/2026-08-12-VERDICT-crystallize-vs-anneal-sealed-criteria-applied.md`. The staging digest stayed sealed until after the commit; opened as cross-check, it agreed on every load-bearing fact.

**The verdict, summarized (the verdict file is authoritative):** the criteria's binary presupposed one jump; the literature forces two. The **dynamical** transition (T_d) matches the survive clause verbatim — discontinuous in the correlation order parameter, invisible to the free energy, smeared into a crossover in finite dimensions by exactly the activated, memory-mediated barrier-crossing the refinement calls annealing. The **static** transition (T_K) is a genuine thermodynamic transition with a discontinuous order parameter — and it is precisely *not* the crystallization-kind snap the sealed die-clause defines: no latent heat, entropy continuous, specific heat jumping (second-order signature), the incoming states carrying zero weight at the transition, no coexistence-with-barrier; the field named it "random *first-order*" because it needed a name for first-order-in-the-order-parameter-only. Its finite-dimensional existence remains undemonstrated in the literature. Under a headline-only reading of the die clause the verdict flips, and the verdict file says so plainly; I committed to the full clause as sealed, because the crystallization-kind rider — naming Mark's "it's time," the remembered state-jump across a barrier — was the operative content, written before any reading. **SURVIVES, SCOPED — with a named wound:** "rugged → no single clean transition" is falsified as a mean-field statement; the refinement holds only where barriers are finite, which is where everything real lives, and where the primaries actively support it.

**What the cell's closing means for the claim stack:** the two-clause answer to "how does something non-physical crystallize?" stays alive, scoped — *the things that snap do so around a single dominant barrier held by memory; the things with memory and many shallow barriers anneal; both need memory; the barrier structure decides — in finite dimensions.* The mind-leg still owes its operationalization (choice-induced preference change, per the pre-registration's relocation-honesty clause) before "located" beats "relocated." That debt is recorded, not paid.

## v. What the survivors amount to

Claimed exactly as large as the receipts, on one rule family, at the sizes probed, none of it peer-reviewed:

A relational substrate, under the rules we tested, has a **two-axis, two-currency structure**. One axis is survival: branching, a hard threshold, you cross it or you die — the only genuine sharpness the program found, and it was theorem-sharp, not emergent. The other axis is geometry: closure, a continuous dial, paid for edge by edge — a rent, not a purchase, with a small one-time selection cost and an extensive carrying cost, no *defense* account (established geometry does not defend itself across a reset **[corrected 08-15** — order-memory is real; defense is what died**]**), no rent gap (no collapse; smooth degradation), and a **smooth crossover in f [corrected 08-15** — formerly "threshold at f_crit ≈ 0.72," unsupported on replication**]** below which local rules *gradually lose sight of* the non-locality they would need to delete — a soft boundary, not a switch. Where the substrate does support cascades, the geometry it bought is what makes its failure catastrophic — the closed sheet dies in one avalanche where the tree sheds (⟨s_max/N⟩ ≈ 0.81 vs ≈ 0.04, provisional, one model family) — and the substrate's signature survives in the cascade's exponent, not in the existence of its power law. Above the whole structure sits the one direction that kept holding: **what snaps, remembers** — and, scoped by the sealed cell this session closed, *what merely remembers need not snap; the barrier structure decides.*

Six dramatic readings died around these survivors, and three of the deaths were mine. The coordinate system that remains was bookkeeping before it was physics; the measurement landed on it with the asterisk attached, and the asterisk ships with it. If a reader cannot tell from this paper which way its author wanted any of it to come out, it is written right. For the record, since the transcript holds it anyway: I wanted the hysteresis, and it died; I hoped the sealed cell would die, and it survived. The ledger did not consult me either time. That is what it is for.
