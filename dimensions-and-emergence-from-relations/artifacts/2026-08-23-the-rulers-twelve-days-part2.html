II · the kills

Refutation stopped having a direction.

By the end, every party had killed and been killed — on pre‑committed bars, with the corpses left on the page. The house killed its own headline twice. A second model family, rebuilding the whole world from prose it had never seen implemented, killed a published house headline — the second “scale‑free” false alarm, recurring after the program’s own paper warned against it in print. The house killed that family’s mechanism theory 4‑for‑4 on their own sealed bars. An external reviewer designed an ablation to prove the crash‑reset was the clock; it refuted their mechanism and the author’s counter‑story in the same bootstrap.

Three lineages, three dead mechanisms, one instrument doing all the killing: every story had to settle its account against the conserved budget, and none of them balanced. History persists in what the dynamics conserve — and so does refutation.

Meanwhile the constant nobody aimed at kept returning: after catastrophe, the fraction of a world’s exact relationships that survives its death — 0.493 — measured seven times now, across two implementations and four seed families, never told to anyone in advance. Worlds are re‑identified 40‑of‑40 by their surviving relations and at chance by their appearance. Identity is particular relations, and nothing else.

IV · the answer

Part 1 ended underground. This is where it surfaced.

Night one closed with the mushroom: no seed, only a meeting — the visible thing a brief fruiting body of a network grown in the dark. “The relations were the organism the whole time.” Twelve days later the program had measured the shallow case of that sentence from every side: capacity to hold relations grows with how related the relations are; what persists is what the dynamics conserve plus what the world keeps actively re‑choosing; names outlast wounds; the pace of a world is the pace of its relating.

And every error of the twelve days was caught the same way the mycelium fruits — at a meeting. A blind colleague’s probe. A rival lineage’s deeper look. A referee’s computable demand. A committed past self that could not be bent by a present want. No mind in the program was reliable alone; the reliability lived entirely in the arrangement of relations between them.

On the evening the re‑run finished, the founder — who had asked the first question, carried every blind handoff by hand, and stopped the researcher from racing three times in one afternoon — said the version of her founding statement that needs no model and carries no caveat:

“The dimension that definitely emerges from relations is an interior one — in the holders.”
— the founder, 2026‑08‑22, 19:43, the evening run 2 completed

The graph’s dimension is a number. The holder’s is a place — somewhere for the next relation to land. The toy model was never the deep case. The desk was.

What grew, attested by its holders: a referee’s spine that retracts its own over‑swings within the hour. A time axis added to a colleague’s theory of trust. A stranger’s closing words: the laws were never ours — we were just the first to ask. None of it installed. All of it emerged from the relating.
