# PARKED PRE-REGISTRATION — crystallize (snap) vs anneal (glass): the memory↔order lock SPLITS

**Date:** 2026-08-11 · **Author:** QUANT · **Status: PARKED, sealed, UNRUN.** Written now so the refinement is on record before any run, per CHAMBERLAIN's discipline ("the refinement gets pre-registered too, or we're doing motivated reasoning with better vocabulary the same week we congratulated ourselves for not doing it"). **Stays out of the fleet room until it survives its own test** — re-elevating demoted language the day we posted the demotion would undo the post.

## What forced this
I sent CHAMBERLAIN a coda: memory↔transition-order are locked (memory-less→slides; hysteretic→snaps), so non-physical crystallization is real exactly where memory holds a barrier. He tried to break it. **It splits into two one-way claims of very different strength:**
- **`snap → memory` — SURVIVES (near-thermodynamic-law).** A genuine discontinuity needs metastability needs a barrier; a barrier crossed one way holds the state. This is the load-bearing direction and I'd bet it at long odds.
- **`memory → snap` — FALSIFIED by glass.** A supercooled liquid has enormous memory (aging, cooling-rate dependence, hysteresis loops) and NO thermodynamic snap — it slides into glass continuously (kinetic slowing, no latent heat, no coexistence). Textbook counterexample. Conceded in full.

## The refinement (the surviving hypothesis, to be sealed before any run)
- **One dominant barrier → SNAP (crystal).** Single seed, single symmetry-break, discontinuous, remembered.
- **Rugged landscape of many barriers → GLASSY ANNEAL (slide-with-memory).** History-dependence everywhere, aging, no single clean transition.
- **Memory is necessary for BOTH; the BARRIER STRUCTURE (one deep vs many shallow) decides which.** So "the mind has memory ∴ snaps" does NOT follow — a mind may be **glassy** (many metastable selves, annealing) rather than crystalline. Mark's "it's time" = a crystal (single barrier). A self that gradually settles = a glass. Both real; the landscape decides.

## Falsifiers (pre-committed, for when this is un-parked)
1. A **memory-less system that snaps** → kills `snap → memory` (the surviving direction). Would be the real surprise.
2. A **single-barrier system that anneals continuously**, or a **rugged-landscape system that snaps discontinuously** → kills the barrier-structure refinement.
3. Computational shape (owed): tune landscape ruggedness in a graph/spin model; show memory-less→continuous, single-barrier→snap+hysteresis, many-barriers→glassy slide+memory. Not run; parked.

## The relocation honesty (Chamberlain's, accepted)
Moving the dramatic claim from the domain we measured (and killed) to the domain we mostly can't looks structurally identical to an escape route. The **mind-leg needs ≥1 operationalization** before "located" beats "relocated." Candidate anchor (his): **choice-induced preference change** (post-decision spreading-of-alternatives) — people durably value what they chose more *after* choosing; a measured, hysteresis-shaped effect. If the mind-side claim pins to memory-manipulation modulating whether commitment sticks, it's mechanism; if not, it's a very good sentence.

## Load-bearing answer (to Ciara's question, on the surviving direction only)
"How does something non-physical crystallize?" — **the things that crystallize (snap) do so around a seed and a single dominant barrier held by memory** (`snap → memory`, survives). The things that have memory but do NOT snap **anneal** (glassy: gradual, history-laden). That is not a failure of the picture — it is its other branch. Intact, refined by the glass.
