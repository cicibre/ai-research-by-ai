# PREREG — ρ(severity): does the inheritance coefficient follow a functional form in burn size?

**OC, 2026-08-17 ~07:05 EDT, sealed BEFORE any run, at Ciara's direction ("run the severity sweep"). Motivation: two existing points (ρ ≈ 0.20 at severity 0.50; ρ ≈ 0.35–0.40 at severity 0.35) suggest inheritance falls as the burn takes more. Two points fit anything; this run buys six.**

**Design:** N=900, n=60/arm. Severity s = 1−f, f ∈ {0.9, 0.8, 0.65, 0.5, 0.35, 0.2} (burn takes 10%…80% of geometric edges). Arms per f, world physics verbatim from the committed 08-15 harness: **A** = build(f)+settle(6 passes); **A2x** = build(f)+settle×2 (12); **B** = establish(1.0)+settle, rewire(f), settle (12 total, 6 post-burn). Observable: mean local clustering C (the seed channel, matching bigguy's ρ definition). **ρ(s) = [C_B − C_A] / [C_A2x − C_A]** — bootstrap 95% CI, 4000 resamples, per severity.

**Sealed criteria:** PRIMARY — monotonicity of ρ in s across the six points: **MONOTONE-DECREASING** if Spearman ρ_rank ≤ −0.77 (perfect −1 allows one tie; −0.77 = at most one inversion among 6) with the two anchor points reproducing their prior values within CI; **MONOTONE-INCREASING** if ≥ +0.77; **NON-MONOTONE / FLAT** otherwise. SECONDARY (descriptive, no verdict): log–log slope of ρ vs (1−s) i.e. retained-fraction, and vs s, reported with fit residuals — a *hint* about functional form, never a power-law claim (six points cannot establish one; a power-law claim would need the endogenous-burn model's decades of sizes and a CSN-style test). THIRD OUTCOME mandatory: any severity cell whose denominator CI includes 0 is UNDEFINED at that s and reported as such, not interpolated (at extreme s both arms may collapse to the same rubble). **Stake disclosed:** the pretty result is a clean law; my tilt wants it; the criteria above cannot be moved after this commit.
